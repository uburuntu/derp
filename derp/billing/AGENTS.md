# Billing — Stars payments

- Owns `purchases`: one receipt per successful Stars payment (pack or subscription cycle), unique on `telegram_charge_id`, never deleted; `status` flips to `refunded`; a chat pack points at the thank-you post that lists it (`patron_post_id`). Also `patron_posts` (see `patrons.py`).
- `purchases.py`: the invoice payload `p1:<product>:<credits>:<user|chat>:<id>[:<topic>]` names exactly what was offered and whose wallet gets it; a chat pack may carry the forum topic its thank-you post goes to, since invoice links are paid in the payer's private chat (payloads stay within Telegram's 128 bytes). Pre-checkout (`refusal`) accepts only offers that still match `Settings.packs` (versioned ids) in XTR, and personal invoices only from the person they were made for; chat invoices anyone may pay. `record_payment` writes the receipt, then credits a `purchase` lot with operation `stars:<charge id>`, in one transaction — a redelivered payment credits nothing — and re-arms the wallet's empty notice (`wallets.empty_noticed_at`). Charge ids have no documented length (131 characters seen), so their columns are wide. A payment whose handler failed pings the owners, and the daily Star transaction sweep (`recover_payments`: an incoming invoice payment's transaction id is its charge id) records any payment without a receipt (credits only; no thank-you post).
- `subscriptions`: Derp+ per user, keyed by its invoice payload `s1:<plan>:<credits>:<user>:<nonce>` (renewals and `subscription` updates carry it). Each paid cycle — first payment or `is_recurring` renewal — is a receipt plus one `plan` lot expiring at `subscription_expiration_date` (no rollover). One running plan per user, enforced when offering and at pre-checkout (Telegram itself allows several; the running plan's own payload passes, in case renewals are pre-checked). `first_charge_id` is what `editUserStarSubscription` needs.
- `menu.py`: `BuyAction` buttons (the only buy callback; `buy_button` is "buy" in private, "top up this chat" in groups) and the menus. Private: the personal menu (Derp+ and pack buttons that post a Stars invoice there); a buy press edits the pressed message into it, keeping that message's other buttons. One builder makes every invoice (posted or as a link); `send_pack_invoice` posts where the Derpcoins go: the chat, or for a personal pack the person's private chat, never a group. Groups: `open_menu` answers a press with an ephemeral message for the presser only (`Target.answering_press`, the press's `callback_query_id`) holding invoice links (`createInvoiceLink`; Derp+ unless one is running). Refused (BadRequest: older than 15 s, or not allowed): a personal menu goes to the private chat, a chat menu replaces the /buy chooser or is posted visibly (chat balances are public).
- `command.py`: `/buy` (private → the personal menu; group → "for this chat" first with the chat's balance, then "just me"), `pre_checkout_query`, `successful_payment` and `subscription` (canceled / active / failed; a canceled plan runs to its period end; a newly failed renewal DMs the subscriber once, with a Subscribe again button). A personal receipt (with the balance) only ever goes to a private chat: paid in a group, the group gets a short thanks without amounts left and the receipt goes by DM. A chat purchase gets the patron post, and a link payer also a receipt in their private chat. Each purchase pings the owners.
- `patrons.py`: one silent post (never pinned) in the chat and topic a chat purchase was made for: buyer name or "a mysterious patron", exact amount, and runway — wallet balance ÷ average daily charged spend of the wallet's runs (`agent_runs.wallet_id`, `charged_credits`) over the days since its first charge in the last 14 (fewer than 3 active days: answers at a typical paid turn). Another chat purchase within 30 minutes edits the same post (`patron_posts`: chat, topic, message; buyers and total are derived from the paid purchases pointing at it, so refunds drop out, and each buyer is named per their current `users.patron_named`). Only a buyer may press Hide my name, within 10 minutes of their purchase (checked on press; a sweep every few minutes drops the expired button): it edits the post and sets `users.patron_named = false` for later purchases. `set_named` is the personal "name me when I chip in" switch (Derp's `change_setting`, later the settings panel). `lately` lists named buyers by amount over 30 days, refunds excluded (/help, /credits).
- `refunds.py`: owner `/refund <charge id>` (unlisted; others get silence) → `refundStarPayment`, then `apply_refund`: purchase `refunded`, unspent remainder of its lot clawed back (operation `refund:<charge>`), spent credits eaten — no debt. Refunding a Derp+ cycle ends it now and cancels its renewal (`stop_renewal` → `editUserStarSubscription`, `is_canceled=True`) on every path: `/refund` (every time, so repeating it retries a cancel that failed), and `refunded_payment` messages and a daily `getStarTransactions` sweep (outgoing transaction to a user with a purchase's charge id = its refund; that chargebacks show up the same way is an assumption the docs don't confirm), which take the same idempotent path and cancel when they first apply the refund. A failed cancel is logged by error class only and pings the owners.
- The billing router sits before the agent handler: payment service messages never invoke Derp.
- Money copy stays plain (no persona; the patron post is the exception, with exact amounts); invoices mention /terms.
