# Settings and user-facing surface

- Owns `/start`, `/help`, `/settings` and per-chat / per-user policy UI. Members can view; only admins mutate chat policy, and the role is re-checked on every mutation.
- `start.py`: `/start` in private is the feature showcase (collapsible sections, Add to group and Buy buttons; Buy turns it into the buy menu, Add to group kept); `/start buy` is the deep link to the buy menu; a group `/start` with a link payload stays silent (the join notice covers it). `/help` describes the chat as it is (how to call Derp, what it sees, today's free and bought Derpcoins in private, the chat wallet and who chipped in lately in groups). `publish_commands` sets the menus per scope and locale at startup — in groups `/help`, `/settings`, `/credits`, `/info`, `/memory`, `/privacy` and `/terms` are ephemeral. In groups `/start`, `/help` and unknown commands are answered only when addressed `@Derp` or sent ephemerally (bare ones may be another bot's); unknown ones get a pointer to `/help` with no model call. A button press no router claims (a button from an older version) gets the out-of-date alert instead of spinning. Owner commands (`OWNER_COMMANDS`, plain English) are listed only for owners: in their private `/help` and in a command menu scoped to their own chat.
- `onboarding.py`: one compact notice per group — when Derp joins (it owns `my_chat_member`, recording membership via `history.members` first) or on the first invocation of a chat that predates it. It states how to summon Derp and what Derp sees (`history.members.context_state`: on, off, or "mentions only" until it's an admin while privacy mode hides messages; `/help` reuses the sentence), and offers the "make me admin" deep link while Derp can't read the chat. The first person's language becomes the chat locale when none is set.
- `pages.py`: `/terms` and `/privacy` (short en/ru pages with collapsible `<details>` sections; the text lives in `locales` under `pages`) and `/paysupport <text>`, which relays the person's own words and id to the owners. Keep both pages true to what the code does when behavior changes (retention, providers, refunds). `PRIVACY.md` at the repo root (the URL BotFather links to) is generated from the privacy page; a test keeps it identical.
- `panel.py`: `/settings` — one panel (ephemeral in groups, edited through `editEphemeralMessage*`): context on/off (off asks first, then purges ambient history via `switch_context`), retention (the choices are `settings.retention.options`, days as `history.retention.kept_days`; a shorter one asks first with a button naming what gets deleted, a longer one applies at once), the time zone (shown only; people set it by telling Derp where they are), notes mode, clearing rules, free models (the chat's switch in groups with a public notice; the person's own consent press in private), and the personal "my Derpcoins in groups" choice (`users.group_spending`). The personal "name me when I chip in" choice (`users.patron_named`) is a panel row too. Pages other than the main one and every chat change re-check admin rights; the personal choices are anyone's own. While Derp can't read the group, the main page carries the make-me-admin link. Confirmation pages (context off, shorter retention) are also what Derp's `change_setting` posts into a chat; pressed outside the ephemeral panel, confirm or Back toasts the outcome and drops the buttons instead of opening anyone's panel there. A public notice (free models on) goes out only when the setting actually changed, so a double tap announces once. `apply_setting` is the one place settings change (panel, `change_setting`, and the patron post's Hide my name); a time-zone change goes through `scheduler.jobs.rezone`.
- The jobs page lists the chat's live jobs (`scheduler.jobs.live_jobs`: chat-local next time, repeat rule, text shown literally, cut to a length budget) with pause/resume and delete buttons for the ones the viewer may touch. Callback data is the job id only; each press goes through `scheduler.jobs.act_on_job`, as does the Resume button on the scheduler's paused-job note (`scheduler.jobs.ResumeJob`, handled here): a resumed note says so and drops the button; one that can't resume any more loses it.
- `privacy.py`: `/forget` (reply to your own message → that message; alone → confirm (the prompt then says what was forgotten), then everything you said in this chat plus your reactions, votes, membership history and personal notes), `switch_context` (the purge behind the context toggle) and the daily `enforce_retention` job. Each also drops stored run transcripts that may quote what was removed (`agent.runs.scrub_runs`); usage and charges stay.
