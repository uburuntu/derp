# Settings and user-facing surface

- Owns `/start`, `/help`, `/settings` and per-chat / per-user policy UI. Members can view; only admins mutate chat policy, and the role is re-checked on every mutation.
- `start.py`: `/start` in private is the feature showcase (collapsible sections, Add to group and Buy buttons; Buy opens a separate personal menu and leaves the showcase and Add to group link intact); `/start buy` is the deep link to the buy menu; a group `/start` with a link payload stays silent (the join notice covers it). `/help` describes the chat as it is (how to call Derp, what it sees, today's free and bought Derpcoins in private, the chat wallet and who chipped in lately in groups). `publish_profile` sets, at startup, Derp's name and descriptions per locale (`locales` `profile`; only when changed, since Telegram rate-limits them), the command list as the menu button, the admin rights Telegram pre-ticks (`history.members.ADMIN_RIGHTS`, the same the make-me-admin link asks for) and the menus per scope and locale — in groups `/help`, `/settings`, `/credits`, `/info`, `/memory`, `/privacy` and `/terms` are ephemeral. In groups `/start`, `/help` and unknown commands are answered only when addressed `@Derp` or sent ephemerally (bare ones may be another bot's); unknown ones get a pointer to `/help` with no model call. A button press no router claims (a button from an older version) gets the out-of-date alert instead of spinning. Owner commands (`OWNER_COMMANDS`, plain English) are listed only for owners: in their private `/help` and in a command menu scoped to their own chat, and in each group an owner is in a member-scoped menu: the group commands plus an ephemeral /context.
- `onboarding.py`: one compact notice per group — when Derp joins (it owns `my_chat_member`, recording membership via `history.members` first) or on the first invocation of a chat that predates it. It states how to summon Derp and what Derp sees (`history.members.context_state`: on, off, or "mentions only" until it's an admin while privacy mode hides messages; `/help` reuses the sentence), and offers the "make me admin" deep link while Derp can't read the chat. The first person's language becomes the chat locale when none is set.
- `pages.py`: `/terms` and `/privacy` (short en/ru pages with collapsible `<details>` sections; the text lives in `locales` under `pages`) and `/paysupport <text>`, which relays the person's own words and id to the owners. Keep both pages true to what the code does when behavior changes (retention, providers, refunds). `PRIVACY.md` at the repo root (the URL BotFather links to) is generated from the privacy page; a test keeps it identical.
- `overview.py`, `sections.py`, `panel.py` and `navigation.py` own /settings. The first card is read-only navigation. Show effective Telegram visibility, retention, automatic replies, the next active scheduled job in chat time, and the available wallet for this scope. Inspecting a page neither opens a wallet nor grants coins. Keep English and Russian copy brief, with an occasional emoji. Memory & history, Rules & replies, Coins & models and Schedule are viewable by any member. My preferences (paid group spending and named patron posts) requires a DM or an ephemeral panel belonging to the viewer. A typed public group panel omits it. Preserve the `set` callback prefix on older cards.
- Check current admin rights on every chat change. Memory & history offers the make-admin link when Telegram restricts delivery. Context off, shorter retention and clearing standing instructions require review; a shorter retention shows the previous and proposed durations. Clear only the standing instructions and pending draft reviewed on the card. Dashboard confirmations lead to receipts with Back and OK. Standalone requests by the agent consume their confirmation and end buttonless. A real switch to group free models posts a public notice. `apply_setting` is shared by the panel, agent and patron actions; a zone change goes through `scheduler.jobs.rezone`.
- Coins & models opens group key management and the signed private key setup link, or the owner's key list in a DM. Key and job management check the actor on each press. The shared overview never includes someone's spending or contribution preferences.
- The jobs page lists the chat's live jobs (`scheduler.jobs.live_jobs`: chat-local next time, repeat rule, text shown literally, cut to a length budget) with pause/resume and delete buttons for the ones the viewer may touch. Successful pause/resume actions show their result with OK/Manage jobs; a cancellation ends without controls. Callback data is the job id only; each press goes through `scheduler.jobs.act_on_job`, as do the Resume button on the scheduler's paused-job note (`scheduler.jobs.ResumeJob`, handled here: a resumed note says so and drops the button; one that can't resume any more loses it) and the Snooze buttons on a fired reminder (`scheduler.jobs.SnoozeJob`: a toast and the buttons dropped; refused for others with the standard alert). Jobs are shown as "job 12", the way the model names them (never `#`, which cites messages).
- `privacy.py`: `/forget` (reply to your own message → that message; alone → confirm (the prompt then says what was forgotten), then everything you said in this chat plus your reactions, votes, membership history and personal notes); admins (anyone in private) also get Forget the whole chat (`ForgetChat`: ask with counts → `forget_whole_chat` via `history.retention.forget_chat`, also clears notes, approvals, automatic replies and their derived jobs; the admin check re-runs on each press; the public prompt becomes the notice naming who did it; standing instructions, independent jobs, games, settings and money stay), `switch_context` (the purge behind the context toggle) and the daily `enforce_retention` job. Each also drops stored run transcripts that may quote what was removed (`agent.runs.scrub_runs`); usage and charges stay.

- Group settings include the paginated automatic-reply rules in `memory.reply_rules`; members see counts and standing instructions; only admins can open the automatic-reply manager and change a rule. Enable, pause and resume show OK/Manage; removing a rule ends its card.

- A `/forget` prompt is requester-bound. Cancel and personal/whole-chat confirmation consume the visible keyboard before reporting the result; a repeated press whose keyboard has already gone cannot wipe later messages. A valid confirmation also reports zero counts.
