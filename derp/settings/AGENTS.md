# Settings and user-facing surface

- Owns `/start`, `/help`, `/settings` and per-chat / per-user policy UI. Members can view; only admins mutate chat policy, and the role is re-checked on every mutation.
- `start.py`: `/start` in private is the feature showcase (collapsible sections, Add to group and Buy buttons); `/start buy` is the deep link to the buy menu; a group `/start` with a link payload stays silent (the join notice covers it). `/help` describes the chat as it is (how to call Derp, what it sees, today's free and bought Derpcoins in private, the chat wallet in groups). `publish_commands` sets the menus per scope and locale at startup — in groups `/help`, `/settings`, `/credits`, `/info`, `/memory`, `/privacy` and `/terms` are ephemeral. In groups `/start`, `/help` and unknown commands are answered only when addressed `@Derp` or sent ephemerally (bare ones may be another bot's); unknown ones get a pointer to `/help` with no model call. Owner commands stay unlisted.
- `onboarding.py`: one compact notice per group — when Derp joins (it owns `my_chat_member`, recording membership via `history.members` first) or on the first invocation of a chat that predates it. It states how to summon Derp, whether Derp can actually see the chat ("mentions only" when ambient is off or privacy mode hides messages), and offers an optional "make me admin" deep link. The first person's language becomes the chat locale when none is set.
- `pages.py`: `/terms` and `/privacy` (short en/ru pages with collapsible `<details>` sections; the text lives in `locales` under `pages`) and `/paysupport <text>`, which relays the person's own words and id to the owners. Keep both pages true to what the code does when behavior changes (retention, providers, refunds).
- `panel.py`: `/settings` — one panel (ephemeral in groups, edited through `editEphemeralMessage*`): context on/off (off asks first, then purges ambient history via `switch_context`), retention, notes mode, clearing rules, free models (the chat's switch in groups with a public notice; the person's consent in private), scheduled count, and the personal "my Derpcoins in groups" choice (`users.group_spending`). Pages other than the main one and every chat change re-check admin rights; the personal choice is anyone's own.
- `privacy.py`: `/forget` (reply to your own message → that message; alone → confirm, then everything you said in this chat plus your reactions, votes, membership history and personal notes), `switch_context` (the purge behind the context toggle) and the daily `enforce_retention` job. Each also drops stored run transcripts that may quote what was removed (`agent.runs.scrub_runs`); usage and charges stay.
